PDF Editor help

Everything happens on your phone. Your PDF is never uploaded.

Quick start
  1. Tap + Add PDF and choose a PDF. You can add more PDFs. Their pages are joined in one list.
  2. The pages appear as small pictures at the bottom. Tap one to see it big.
  3. Fix the order with ◀ Earlier and Later ▶, or type a place number and tap Go.
  4. Tap 💾 Save PDF. The new PDF goes to Downloads.
Move, turn and delete pages
  • ⟳ Turn turns the page a quarter turn. Do this before you write on the page.
  • ✕ Delete page removes the page. ↩ Undo brings it back.
  • Delete pages from … to … removes many pages at once.
Write names, dates and numbers
  • Tap ➕ Add text, type, and choose a handwriting style, a colour and a size.
  • Drag the text on the page to put it where you want. Tap a text to change it.
  • 📅 Add today's date writes today's date.
  • ⇉ Copy to all pages puts the same text in the same place on every page.
  • Number the pages puts a page number on every page. The numbers follow the new order.
  • Numbers look best in Dancing Script, Caveat, Satisfy, Pacifico and Homemade Apple.
Quality
  • Pages are not turned into pictures. Their original content is kept exactly, so the resolution and quality stay the same.
  • Text you add is drawn as a very sharp picture of the letters on top of the page. It prints sharp, but it is not searchable text.
Limits and fixes
  • Very big PDFs (hundreds of MB) may not fit in the phone memory. Work with smaller PDFs.
  • Locked (password) PDFs cannot be opened.
  • Your edits are not kept if you close the page. Save first.
  • If a PDF will not open, add it again from the Files app.

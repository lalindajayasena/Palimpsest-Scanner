Help

Everything runs on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Quick start
  1. Tap + Add photos and choose your book photos (or 📷 Take photo).
  2. For two-page photos, tap Split all.
  3. Tap a small photo at the bottom to see it big at the top. Swipe the big photo left or right to go to the next one.
  4. Fix the order with ◀ Earlier and Later ▶, or type a place number and tap Go.
  5. Tap 👁 Finished look to see how the page will look in the PDF.
  6. Choose the language, then tap Make PDF.
  7. Keep the screen open. Your files go to the Downloads folder.
Buttons on each photo
  • ⟳ Turn turns the photo.
  • ◀ Earlier / Later ▶ move the photo in the order. Go moves it to a place number.
  • 👁 shows the finished page.
  • 🖌 lets you paint over fingers or marks.
  • ✕ removes the photo from the list (not from your phone).
  • Split into 2 pages cuts a two-page photo down the middle.
Settings explained
  • Look: Natural keeps paper colour. Clean is grey and white. Strong black and white is best for faded print. Original changes nothing.
  • Set crop and tilt for all photos: the most reliable way to remove the table and fingers at the edges. Choose a typical photo, drag the sliders until the green box fits the page, then Done. The same crop is used for every photo, so photograph all pages the same way.
  • Shadow removal strong: use it when the edges of shadows are still visible. It reads the light in finer detail and runs two times. Letters and pictures are not changed, only the uneven light.
  • Do not remove shadows on this photo: tick it for a page that is mostly a big picture, so the picture keeps its dark parts.
  • Picture quality: Normal keeps pages about 2000 pixels tall. High keeps about 3000 and saves with less compression. High makes bigger PDFs and is slower, and may be too heavy for a very old phone.
  • Auto-crop (experimental): finds the page edges in the photo, cuts away the table, and straightens a tilted page. Tap 👁 to check the green outline. Tick Skip auto-crop on a photo where it goes wrong. It works best when the page is brighter than what is behind it. It does not flatten a curved page near the spine.
  • Remove shadows: evens out the light. Turn it off if the page has big pictures.
  • Trim edges: cuts a few percent from every side. Use 3 to remove fingers at the edge.
  • Read the text (OCR): makes the PDF searchable and saves a .txt file. It is slow, about 15 to 40 seconds a page.
  • Language: pick the language of the book. The first time, extra data downloads and needs internet.
  • Chapter bookmarks: makes a chapter list inside the PDF. Check the report below the button.
  • Pages in each part: smaller is safer for your phone. 40 is good.
  • What to save: one giant PDF, separate parts, or both.
Hundreds of photos: tips
  • Test with 5 photos first. Check the look, the text and the report.
  • Plug in the charger and keep the screen on. Do not switch to other apps.
  • If your phone struggles, do 50 to 100 photos at a time and save Separate PDF parts. Join them later with Combine PDFs.
  • If Chrome asks about downloading many files, tap Allow.
Reader (read and listen)
  • Open it from the link at the top. New OCR books appear there by themselves.
  • It shows large text, three colour themes, chapters, and can read aloud.
  • Listening needs the screen to stay on.
Install as an app (works offline)
  • Tap ⬇ Install app if you see it. Or open the Chrome menu (three dots) and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Then it opens from your home screen like a normal app.
  • After you have used it once with internet, it can open without internet. OCR language data may need one online use first.
Problems and fixes
  • Photo could not be read or opened: use 📁 Add from Files instead of + Add photos. If a photo is in Google Photos, open it in the Gallery first so it downloads. Also check Settings, Apps, Chrome, Permissions, Photos and videos.
  • Photos are saved inside the app, so they are still here next time. Tap Clear all when you are finished, to free space on your phone.
  • Page stops or restarts: the phone ran out of memory. Use a smaller part size, like 20.
  • No bookmarks: the book may have no contents page or large headings. The PDF is still fine.
  • Text has mistakes: try Strong black and white, and photograph the pages with even light.
  • New version not showing: close the app and open it again, or refresh the page.
